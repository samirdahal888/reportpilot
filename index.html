The problem

Winning the grant was the easy part.

Then come the reports. Every funder wants proof of how you spent their money and what it achieved, in their own format, on their own schedule.

  • The numbersSpending sits in QuickBooks under your general categories. Someone splits it across each grant's budget lines in Excel, by hand, every time.
  • The storyThe same program story gets rewritten for six different templates, word limits and portals.
  • The outcomesAttendance and results live in a different spreadsheet, kept by a different person, who has to be chased.
  • The deadlinesDue dates are buried in award letters and someone's inbox. A late report can cost next year's grant.

How it works

From award letter to finished draft

  1. Connect your data

    Link QuickBooks and upload your program spreadsheet or outcomes tracker. Takes about 10 minutes.

  2. Add your grants

    Upload each award letter. ReportPilot reads the reporting requirements, budget lines and due dates.

  3. Get a draft

    Two weeks before each deadline, you get a draft in that funder's format: narrative, numbers and budget-vs-actual.

  4. Review and submit

    Edit anything, export to Word or PDF, or paste into the funder's portal.
